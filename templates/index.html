7. Research Finding

Logistic Regression achieved the highest standalone classification accuracy of 97.49%.

The proposed Hybrid ML + RAG framework achieved 97.45% accuracy while additionally providing retrieval-based resolution support and confidence-aware decision making.

8. Proposed Research Contribution

The proposed system combines ML intent classification, FAISS-based RAG retrieval and confidence-aware decision making for e-commerce customer-support complaints.

Complaints with reliable retrieval matches can use the RAG path, supported complaints can use the ML classifier, and unsupported or low-confidence complaints can be sent for manual review.

For this demonstration, the framework is presented as a Flipkart e-commerce customer-support use case.
