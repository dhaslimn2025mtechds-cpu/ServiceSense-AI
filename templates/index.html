7. Research Finding

Logistic Regression achieved 97.49% classification accuracy.

The proposed Hybrid ML + RAG framework achieved 97.45% accuracy and 97.45% F1 score.

The Hybrid framework provides comparable classification performance while additionally supporting retrieval-based resolution suggestion, similarity validation and confidence-aware decision making.

Therefore, the proposed research focuses on improving the practical reliability of customer complaint resolution rather than only maximizing classification accuracy.
