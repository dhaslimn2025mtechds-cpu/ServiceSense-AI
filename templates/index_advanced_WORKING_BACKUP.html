6. Proposed Research Components

Semantic Classification MiniLM sentence embeddings are used with Logistic Regression for intent prediction.
Hybrid Retrieval BM25 sparse retrieval and MiniLM dense FAISS retrieval provide complementary evidence.
RRF Fusion Reciprocal Rank Fusion combines sparse and dense retrieval rankings.
Cross-Encoder Re-ranking Retrieved candidates are re-ranked using a Cross-Encoder relevance model.
Uncertainty Awareness Low-confidence or conflicting predictions trigger clarification or escalation instead of automatically forcing a resolution.
LangGraph Multi-Turn Agent Conversation state controls resolution, satisfaction checking, follow-up handling and human escalation.

7. Proposed Research Contribution

ServiceSense AI extends single-turn complaint classification into a stateful multi-turn resolution framework. The system combines semantic classification, hybrid evidence retrieval, uncertainty-aware decisions and agentic workflow orchestration.

Key workflow: Customer Complaint → Classify → Retrieve Evidence → Re-rank → Resolve or Clarify → Check Satisfaction → Follow-Up → Re-resolve or Human Escalation
