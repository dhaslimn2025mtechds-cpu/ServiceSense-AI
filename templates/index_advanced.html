Research Components

Core methods used in the proposed ServiceSense AI framework.

03
SC
Semantic Classification MiniLM sentence embeddings with Logistic Regression for customer intent prediction.
HR
Hybrid Retrieval BM25 sparse retrieval and MiniLM FAISS dense retrieval provide complementary evidence.
RF
RRF Fusion Reciprocal Rank Fusion combines sparse and dense retrieval rankings.
CE
Cross-Encoder Re-ranking Candidate evidence is re-ranked using query-document relevance.
UA
Uncertainty Awareness Low-confidence or conflicting predictions trigger clarification rather than forcing an unreliable resolution.
LG
LangGraph Agent Stateful orchestration manages resolution, satisfaction checks, follow-ups and human escalation.
